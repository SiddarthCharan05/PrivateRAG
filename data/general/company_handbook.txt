1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

NordWerk Solutions GmbH is a fictional German technology company specialising in industrial automation, data analytics and enterprise software solutions.

The company employs approximately 500 employees across offices in Berlin, Hamburg and Munich.

2. WORKING HOURS

The standard working week is 40 hours from Monday to Friday.

Employees may use flexible working hours between 07:00 and 19:00, subject to departmental requirements.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00.

3. REMOTE WORK

Eligible employees may work remotely for up to three days per week.

Remote work must be approved by the employee's line manager.

Employees working remotely must use company-managed devices and the approved corporate VPN when accessing internal systems.

4. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE USAGE

Employees may use AI tools approved by the IT and Data Protection teams for business purposes.

Confidential company information, personal employee data, customer information, financial information and authentication credentials must not be submitted to unapproved public AI services.

Where sensitive company information is required for an AI-assisted process, employees should use approved internally hosted AI systems.

5. INFORMATION SECURITY

Employees must immediately report suspected data breaches, phishing attempts or loss of company equipment to the IT Security team.

6. DOCUMENT CONFIDENTIALITY

This handbook is classified as INTERNAL and may be accessed by all NordWerk employees.
