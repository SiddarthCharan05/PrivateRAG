NORDWERK SOLUTIONS GMBH
AI AND INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

Document Classification: CONFIDENTIAL
Version: 1.3
Effective Date: 15 February 2026
Document Owner: IT Security

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines security requirements for the use of artificial intelligence systems at NordWerk Solutions GmbH.

2. APPROVED AI ENVIRONMENT

Sensitive company information may only be processed using AI systems approved by NordWerk IT Security.

For confidential workloads, preference must be given to internally hosted or otherwise approved controlled AI environments.

3. PROHIBITED INFORMATION

Employees must not submit the following information to unapproved public AI services:

- Customer personal information
- Employee personal information
- Financial account information
- Passwords or authentication credentials
- API keys
- Confidential contracts
- Proprietary source code classified as confidential
- Internal security documentation

4. PERSONAL DATA

Personal data must be processed only for an authorised business purpose and according to applicable organisational and data-protection requirements.

Data minimisation should be applied wherever possible.

5. AI OUTPUT

AI-generated output must not automatically be assumed to be correct.

Employees remain responsible for reviewing AI-generated information before it is used for business decisions.

6. INCIDENT REPORTING

Suspected disclosure of confidential information through an AI service must be reported immediately to IT Security.

7. ACCESS

This document is restricted to authorised IT and security personnel.

END OF DOCUMENT
